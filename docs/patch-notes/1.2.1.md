# Cookie Jar 1.2.1

- Date: 2026-10-28
- Platform: Windows

## Briefing

Fix launching a second Roblox client when a hidden Roblox tray client swallows the launch.

## Changes

- Before every launch (website launch and saved-account launch) the launcher now ends hidden Roblox tray clients: `RobloxPlayerBeta` processes with no visible window that either carry `--launch-to-tray` or are more than a minute old. Roblox 0.744 keeps such a process resident (autostart entry, and closing a window leaves it in the tray) and hands new `roblox-player:` launches to it instead of starting a second client, which showed up as only one game running at a time. The activity log reports how many were ended.
- Clients with a visible window, including minimised ones, are never touched.

## Source

- `src/InstanceManager.cs`: `RobloxProcesses.EndIdleTrayClients`, called from `Launch` and `LaunchAccount`.

## Validation

- Windows build only. Diagnosis comes from a Roblox 0.744 player log, the 0.744 and 0.745 client strings and the Roblox registry entries on a development PC. No live multi-launch test was run, and the original report could not be reproduced.

## Known limitations

- Unconfirmed as the cause of the reported problem. The reporter's log showed their 0.744 client handing the launch to the updater for 0.745 and exiting; their behaviour after updating is unknown. The 0.745 client still uses the same `ROBLOX_singletonEvent`/`ROBLOX_singletonMutex` names and `SingleInstanceGuard`, as 0.744 does.
- A client whose window was closed and sits hidden in the tray is ended on the next launch.
